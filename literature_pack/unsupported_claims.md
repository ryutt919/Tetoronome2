# Unsupported or Downshifted Claims

These claims should not appear in a top-tier manuscript unless new direct evidence is added.

## Remove

- Heiderscheit et al. directly supports "impact increases when stride-length adjustment fails."
- Collins 2009 supports auditory cueing or plantarflexion/push-off activation.
- Thaut 1993 directly supports running plantarflexion or running push-off increase.
- IMU resultant acceleration-time integral is equivalent to GRF impulse.
- Taping improves proprioception and therefore improves step regularity.

## Downshift

- "Metronome caused stronger push-off."
  - Use: "Metronome condition was associated with increased ankle plantarflexion peak, suggesting ankle posture/strategy change."

- "Taping reduced impact."
  - Use: "Taping condition showed a small, non-significant reduction in IMU shock proxy."

- "Metronome impaired stability."
  - Use: "Metronome did not improve pelvis SD or step interval CV in this sample; T->T+M increased CV significantly."

- "Combined condition offset taping's shock benefit by strong landing/push-off."
  - Use: "The combined condition did not preserve the small shock-proxy reduction seen in taping alone; mechanism remains exploratory."

## Needs direct evidence before use

- ankle power
- plantarflexor moment
- propulsive impulse
- gastrocnemius/soleus EMG in the present protocol
- GRF vertical loading rate
- foot strike classification
- validated IMU-GRF equivalence
