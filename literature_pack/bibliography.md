# Bibliography and Use Boundaries

## Direct / near-direct support

1. Heiderscheit BC, Chumanov ES, Michalski MP, Wille CM, Ryan MB. Effects of step rate manipulation on joint mechanics during running. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise. 2011.
   - URL: https://uwnmbl.engr.wisc.edu/pubs/acsm10.pdf
   - Local file: `03_contrast_evidence/heiderscheit_2011_step_rate_running.pdf`
   - Use: metronome/step-rate manipulation and contrast for shock proxy results.

2. Chinn L, Dicharry J, Hertel J. Ankle kinematics of individuals with chronic ankle instability while walking and jogging on a treadmill in shoes. Journal of Athletic Training. 2014.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4080591/
   - Local file: link-only, PMC PDF download returned HTML challenge.
   - Use: ankle taping can alter ankle kinematics. Do not use for shock absorption or proprioception.

3. Cordova ML et al. Ankle support/taping ROM evidence.
   - URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK68419/
   - Local file: link-only.
   - Use: ROM restriction / mechanical support.

## Mechanistic support

4. Yong JR, Silder A, Montgomery KL, Fredericson M, Delp SL. Differences in muscle activity between natural forefoot and rearfoot strikers during running. Journal of Biomechanics. 2014.
   - URL: https://nmbl.stanford.edu/publications/pdf/Yong2014.pdf
   - Local file: `02_mechanistic_support/yong_2014_forefoot_rearfoot_emg.pdf`
   - Use: plantarflexed ankle posture and gastrocnemius activity differ by running strike strategy.

5. Hamner SR, Seth A, Delp SL. Muscle contributions to propulsion and support during running. Journal of Biomechanics. 2010.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2973845/
   - Local file: link-only, PMC PDF download returned HTML challenge.
   - Use: background mechanism that plantarflexors contribute to propulsion. Do not use to claim PF angle equals push-off power.

6. Thaut MH et al. Effect of rhythmic auditory cuing on temporal stride parameters and EMG patterns in hemiparetic gait of stroke patients. 1993.
   - URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/136140969300700103
   - Local file: link-only, DNS failure during download.
   - Use: auditory cueing and temporal/EMG pattern background only.

## Contrast / caution

7. Raymond J et al. The effect of ankle taping or bracing on proprioception in functional ankle instability: a systematic review and meta-analysis. 2012.
   - URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22513304/
   - Local file: link-only.
   - Use: caution against proprioception mechanism claims.

8. Terrier P, Deriaz O. Persistent and anti-persistent pattern in stride-to-stride variability of treadmill walking: influence of rhythmic auditory cueing. 2012.
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4586
   - Local file: link-only, DNS failure during download.
   - Use: indirect background on rhythmic cueing and stride timing variability.

## Methods / proxy caution

9. IMU/tibial acceleration methods source.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10820910/
   - Local file: link-only, PMC direct PDF failed.
   - Use: general caution that wearable acceleration outcomes are proxies and depend on placement/processing. Verify exact paper before using as a primary methods citation.

## A as to B upgrade sources

10. Farley CT, Gonzalez O. Leg stiffness and stride frequency in human running. Journal of Biomechanics. 1996.
   - URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8849811/
   - Use: supports the mechanism that stride frequency changes spring-like leg stiffness.

11. Kulmala JP et al. Running in highly cushioned shoes increases leg stiffness and amplifies impact loading. Scientific Reports. 2018.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-35980-6
   - Use: supports the link between increased leg stiffness and amplified impact loading.

12. Schubert AG, Kempf J, Heiderscheit BC. Influence of stride frequency and length on running mechanics: a systematic review. Sports Health. 2014.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4000471/
   - Use: supports stride-rate effects on running mechanics and more plantarflexed ankle position framing.

13. Williams DS et al. Changes in lower extremity movement and power absorption during forefoot striking and barefoot running. International Journal of Sports Physical Therapy. 2012.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3474309/
   - Use: supports foot/ankle demand and ankle power absorption in forefoot-like running mechanics.

14. Cronstrom A et al. Metronome cueing and stride time variability.
   - URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35020171/
   - Use: supports that metronome cueing can increase stride time variability.

15. Terrier P, Deriaz O. Persistent and anti-persistent pattern in stride-to-stride variability of treadmill walking: influence of rhythmic auditory cueing. PLoS ONE. 2012.
   - URL: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0047171
   - Use: supports that rhythmic auditory cueing reorganizes stride-to-stride variability dynamics.
