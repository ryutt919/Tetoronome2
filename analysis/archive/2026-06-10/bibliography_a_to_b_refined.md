# Bibliography For A→B Refined Version

작성일: 2026-06-10

## Strong mechanistic support

1. Farley CT, González O. Leg stiffness and stride frequency in human running. Journal of Biomechanics. 1996.
   - Use: stride frequency and spring-like leg stiffness mechanism.
   - URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8849811/

2. Kulmala JP, Kosonen J, Nurminen J, Avela J. Running in highly cushioned shoes increases leg stiffness and amplifies impact loading. Scientific Reports. 2018.
   - Use: leg stiffness and impact loading amplification connection.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-35980-6

3. Schubert AG, Kempf J, Heiderscheit BC. Influence of stride frequency and length on running mechanics: a systematic review. Sports Health. 2014.
   - Use: stride-rate changes running mechanics and ankle posture.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4000471/

4. Yong JR, Silder A, Montgomery KL, Fredericson M, Delp SL. Differences in muscle activity between natural forefoot and rearfoot strikers during running. Journal of Biomechanics. 2014.
   - Use: forefoot strike, plantarflexed ankle posture, and gastrocnemius activity.
   - URL: https://nmbl.stanford.edu/publications/pdf/Yong2014.pdf

5. Williams DSB, Green DH, Wurzinger B. Changes in lower extremity movement and power absorption during forefoot striking and barefoot running. International Journal of Sports Physical Therapy. 2012.
   - Use: forefoot/barefoot running and foot/ankle demand.
   - URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3474309/

## Direct / near-direct gait support with limited transfer

6. Cronström A, et al. Acute effect of traditional and adaptive metronomes on gait variability in older adults with a history of falls. Ageing Clinical and Experimental Research. 2022.
   - Use: metronome cueing can increase stride time variability in a specific gait population/context.
   - Limitation: older adults/history of falls; not identical to current running+taping protocol.
   - URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35020171/

7. Terrier P, Deriaz O. Persistent and anti-persistent pattern in stride-to-stride variability of treadmill walking: influence of rhythmic auditory cueing. PLoS ONE. 2012.
   - Use: rhythmic auditory cueing can reorganize stride-to-stride variability dynamics.
   - URL: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0047171

## Constraint and caution sources retained

8. Chinn L, Dicharry J, Hart JM, Saliba S, Wilder R, Hertel J. Gait kinematics after taping in participants with chronic ankle instability. Journal of Athletic Training. 2014.
   - Use: taping-related ankle kinematic constraint.

9. Cordova ML, Ingersoll CD, LeBlanc MJ. Influence of ankle support on joint range of motion before and after exercise: a meta-analysis. JOSPT. 2000.
   - Use: ankle support / ROM restriction.

10. Raymond J, Nicholson M, Hiller CE, Refshauge KM. The effect of ankle taping or bracing on proprioception in functional ankle instability: a systematic review and meta-analysis. JSAMS. 2012.
   - Use: caution against proprioception-improvement claims.
