Running analysis live IMU platform

Connect three DOT sensors placed on the shank, foot, and hip. All three DOTs stream quaternion orientation with free acceleration. The live view estimates ankle dorsiflexion/plantarflexion and inversion/eversion from the zeroed foot-shank relative quaternion, while hip pitch/roll are estimated from the zeroed hip quaternion.
